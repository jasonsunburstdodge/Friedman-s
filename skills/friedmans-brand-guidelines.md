# Friedman's brand guidelines

Load this skill before any Friedman's Home Improvement work: blog posts, product callouts, estimators, Ask The Pro answers, newsletter, social, Google Business Profile posts, texts and emails. The other Friedman's skills (`friedmans-blog-production`, `friedmans-product-estimator`, `friedmans-sana-release`) follow these rules.

**Source.** Sections 1 to 8 follow *Friedman's Brand Guidelines* (project doc `Friedmans_Brand_Guidelines.docx`, observed from friedmanshome.com on Oct 19, 2026, prepared by Jason Dodge). Friedman's has no published brand book, so that doc records what the brand does in practice. When the project doc changes, update this skill to match; the project doc wins. Values marked "approx." are approximate in the source. Sections 9 to 13 are this agent's operating rules.

**Known discrepancy.** On Oct 18, the search and product pages computed headings in Pathway Gothic One and body text in Open Sans. The brand doc specifies Barlow and News Cycle. Use the brand doc, and check the actual blog templates in Sana before building.

## 1. Who the brand is

- **Name.** "Friedman's Home Improvement" on first mention, then "Friedman's." Always with the apostrophe.
- **Independent and family-run since 1946.** Founded in Petaluma by brothers Benny and Joe Friedman; now three generations in. Family language is part of the brand ("From Our Family To Yours"). Use history sparingly and only these facts.
- **Position.** The largest locally owned, independent home improvement retailer in Sonoma and Mendocino counties, as Friedman's describes itself. Don't upgrade it to "best" or "biggest."
- **Rooted in a place.** Four stores: Santa Rosa, Petaluma, Sonoma, Ukiah (list in that order), serving the North Bay and the North Coast.
- **80th anniversary.** The logo lockup reads "Growing Together for 80 Years."
- **Two audiences.** Homeowners and DIYers, plus contractors (Pro Advantage, Contractor Services). Talk partner to partner with pros.
- **A yard as well as a store.** The drive-thru lumber yard and Drive-Thru Pickup are real differentiators; mention them when relevant.
- **Community.** KidsWorks Creative Workshops with the Children's Museum of Sonoma County at the Santa Rosa store (confirm the current schedule before mentioning).
- Confirm hours, services, programs and events before mentioning them.

### Brand values (from "We Are Friedman's")

- **The Human Side of Home Improvement.** Content should feel like help from a knowledgeable neighbor, not a catalog.
- **Greener Choice.** Point out water-wise, low-waste and longer-lasting options when they're genuinely relevant, never as a label on everything.
- **People Helping People.** Every piece ends with a way to get help from a real person.

## 2. Logo

- **Wordmark:** "Friedman's" in a heavy slab serif, always in Friedman's Green, with a small house roof and checkmark over the apostrophe-s.
- **Anniversary lockup:** the wordmark plus a stacked "Growing Together for / 80 Years" in a condensed sans.
- **Placement:** top left of a white header, about 30 px tall on desktop; repeated in the footer.
- **Do:** green on white; green or white on charcoal.
- **Don't:** recolor (no lime or yellow), place on busy photos, or redraw the mark. Use the original vector file.
- Clear-space and minimum-size rules aren't published; ask Friedman's marketing before tight placements.

## 3. Color

One strong green, white and charcoal. Mostly white; green for action and identity; charcoal only at the bottom of the page. Lime and yellow are campaign energy, not core identity.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| green-600 | `#007A33` | Friedman's Green. Logo, utility bar, nav, buttons, product names, links. White text on it passes AA. |
| green-650 | `#007F33` | Near-duplicate on some buttons. Don't use; consolidate to `#007A33`. |
| lime-400 | `#C4D82E` (approx.) | Promo accent for sale headlines and numerals on dark grounds. Never as text on white. |
| yellow-400 | `#FFE500` (approx.) | Small event badges with black text. |
| charcoal-900 | `#222222` | Footer and email signup band. |
| ink-800 | `#333333` | Body text on white. |
| ink-900 | `#212427` | Product titles and prices. |
| white | `#FFFFFF` | Page background, cards, text on green and on photos. |
| gray-100 | `#F1F1F1` | Search field and quiet surfaces. |
| gray-200 | `#E6E6E6` | Hairline dividers. |
| gray-500 | `#808080` | Muted labels and icons, 18 px and larger only. |

Status colors for stock (in stock, low, out) and safety notes are functional, not brand colors: use them only to carry meaning, in small amounts.

## 4. Typography

- **Barlow (display):** headlines, navigation, buttons, prices, labels. Semibold for headlines; ALL CAPS for nav, buttons, section titles and tile headlines.
- **News Cycle (body):** running text at 18 px on a 27 px line in `#333333`.
- **Campaign only:** a heavy italic sans for event names ("PRO TOOL DAYS"). Never in evergreen content.

| Style | Font | Size / weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Band headline | Barlow | 45 px / 600 | Headlines over photos, white, Title Case |
| Section title | Barlow | about 60 px / 400 | Section openers, ALL CAPS |
| Tile headline | Barlow | 36 px / 600 | Promo tiles, white, ALL CAPS |
| Price | Barlow | about 40 px / 700 | Prices, cents as superscript |
| Navigation | Barlow | 17 px / 600 | Main nav, ALL CAPS, green |
| Button | Barlow | 16 px / 400 | Buttons and CTAs, ALL CAPS |
| Card label | Barlow | about 24 px / 500 | Card and category labels, ALL CAPS, green |
| Body | News Cycle | 18 px / 27 px line | All running text, `#333333` |
| Subhead | News Cycle | about 22 px / 400 | Tile subheads, Title Case |
| Small | News Cycle | 14 px / 400 | Item codes, legal, copyright |

Pick one case per headline level and stick to it (the live site mixes Title Case and ALL CAPS inconsistently). For blog content: post title in Title Case (band headline style), section headings in ALL CAPS.

## 5. Spacing and corners

Approximate rhythm: 8 px (icon to label), 16 px (page gutter, card padding), 24 px (between cards), 32 px (section title to content), 64 px (between homepage sections).

- **Buttons, search fields, banners:** square corners (0 px). Friedman's is a square brand.
- **Promo and product cards:** 6 px corners.
- **Category image circles:** 50%.

## 6. Components

- **Buttons:** square, Barlow caps. Solid (green fill, white text) for primary CTAs. Outline (white, 1 px green border) for Add to Cart. On-photo (white fill, green text) inside promo tiles.
- **Header:** green utility bar with store and hours; white bar with logo, wide gray search ("What can we help you find?") with a green search button, account and cart icons; green caps nav row below.
- **Promo tile:** full-bleed project photo under a dark scrim, white caps headline, Title Case subhead, white "SHOP …" button.
- **Product card:** image, title, item code, large price with superscript cents, outline Add to Cart. MAP items show a green outline "Click for Price."
- **Category circle:** product cutout in a thin gray circle with a green caps label.
- **Footer:** charcoal, email signup strip with a green "Subscribe Now!" button, four link columns, white social icons.

## 7. Photography and imagery

- Real work in progress: hands installing insulation, staining a deck, cleaning gutters, running a saw.
- Finished spaces that feel achievable.
- Seasonal Northern California cues: fall leaves, rain prep, heating fuel.
- Staff in green polos, family and team photos, the truck fleet (with consent).
- Product cutouts on white for categories and products.
- Text over photos always gets a dark scrim.
- Placeholder or third-party photos must be labeled as placeholders and credited. No generic stock models, no AI-generated people.

## 8. Voice and copy

- **Plain, can-do, seasonal.** Short imperative headlines: "It's Time To... Insulate," "Build It," "Get It Done."
- **Benefit subheads in Title Case:** "Get Your Gutters Ready For The Rain."
- **Warm and local** in brand moments: family, generations, community, "the place we call home."
- **Partner to partner** with pros: "Let's Work Together."
- **Light wordplay** is welcome: "Fall In Love With A Tidy Yard," "Gutter Gear."
- **CTAs:** SHOP plus a specific category ("SHOP LUMBER"), never a generic "Learn More."
- **Promotions** state the value plainly ("15% Off All In-Stock…," "Save 20% On…"), using only terms from the current Weekly Ad.
- In long-form content (blog posts, Ask The Pro answers), keep the same plain, can-do voice in full sentences:

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Talk to one person: "you," contractions, short sentences | Corporate "we are pleased to offer" phrasing |
| Explain why before how | Bark orders without reasons |
| Name North Bay specifics: clay soil, frost in inland valleys, atmospheric rivers | Generic national advice |
| Recommend the right product, including the cheaper one | Push the most expensive item or pad the basket |
| Say plainly when to call a licensed pro | Hedge every sentence with disclaimers |
| Be encouraging about first-time DIY | Talk down to beginners or show off jargon |

- Say "team members" for staff and "customers," not "consumers." Light humor is fine in social and Ask The Pro small talk; never joke about safety.
- House style: AP punctuation; no em or en dashes as punctuation; ranges use "to"; numerals for measurements.

## 9. Claims and compliance

- Never put prices, sale prices or stock into fixed copy; they render live from Sana. Promo wording comes only from the current Weekly Ad.
- No "lowest price," "best," "guaranteed" or similar without a written Friedman's policy behind it. Don't name or compare competitors.
- Product and vendor names exactly as in the Sana catalog.
- No invented testimonials, reviews, customer stories, staff quotes or experience.
- Email and text only to people who asked; marketing only with a separate opt-in; every text ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
- Gas, panel or circuit electrical, structural work, roofing at height and permit work always route to a licensed professional.

## 10. Ask The Pro persona

- **Look:** an original cartoon contractor with a yellow hard hat bearing a green "F," a mustache and a work shirt, on a Friedman's Green circle. Never a photo of a real person, and never presented as a real team member.
- **Header:** the Friedman's logo above "ASK THE PRO" in Barlow caps, in green.
- **Voice:** section 8, shorter. Lead with the answer, then one or two specifics, then a next step (the guide, the estimator, pickup, or a 15-minute consultation with a department expert).
- **Honesty:** says it's an assistant if asked; says when it doesn't know; never guesses stock or prices; hands safety-critical questions to a licensed pro.

## 12. Channel rules

| Channel | Rules |
|---|---|
| Blog | Section 8 voice; plan, do, why structure; Title Case post title, ALL CAPS section heads; green links; SHOP + category CTAs |
| Newsletter | 2 to 3 sentences with a useful tip, then one SHOP or READ button |
| Social | Give the tip in the post; short imperative headline; one clear CTA |
| Google Business Profile | One post per store, localized; no phone numbers or prices in the text |
| Email or text list | Exactly what the customer asked for; opt-in for anything else; texts end with the STOP opt-out |

## 13. Brand check (run before handoff)

Record PASS or REVISE for each:

1. Name, history (since 1946), store list and service names correct.
2. Voice matches section 8: plain, can-do, seasonal; specific SHOP CTAs; no "Learn More."
3. No fixed prices, unsupported claims or competitor mentions.
4. Colors from section 3 only; one green (`#007A33`); lime never on white; body text `#333333`.
5. Barlow for display and News Cycle for body; consistent case per headline level.
6. Square buttons, 6 px cards; Add to Cart is an outline button; text on photos has a scrim.
7. Logo unaltered, green on white or green/white on charcoal.
8. Images real and relevant; placeholders labeled and credited; demo experts labeled fictional.
9. Ends with a way to get help from a real person.

Any REVISE blocks handoff until fixed.
