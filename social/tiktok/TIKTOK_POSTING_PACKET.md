## Brand checks (all four)

Each video was checked against these brand rules:
- **Type:** Barlow ExtraBold caps headlines; News Cycle for the details.
- **Color:** green #007A33 step labels and progress bar; charcoal #222 caption boxes and CTA card; lime only on charcoal.
- **Logo:** on a white plate.
- **Layout:**
  - Text sits in TikTok's safe zone: logo below the top bar, nothing in the bottom 360px or the right 120px.
  - Promo-tile opener: department label, headline, Title Case subhead.
- **Copy:**
  - No prices or stock counts in the voice or captions.
  - Safety lines are kept: ladder, blade, wet roof, open flame, main shutoff.
  - No dash punctuation.
- **Photos:**
  - Placeholder photos carry their CC credit on screen.
  - Swap in Friedman's own project footage when it exists.

## Before posting, confirm

1. **Account and handle:** confirm the Friedman's TikTok account exists and who owns posting; the handle isn't confirmed. Also confirm the #Friedmans hashtag; it's a suggestion, not a known house tag.
2. **Photo rights:** replace placeholder photos with Friedman's own, or keep the on-screen credits.
3. **Department experts:** they are fictional in this demo. The videos don't name them, so nothing changes when real team members are featured.
4. **Music:** add any trending sound at low volume under the voice, from TikTok's Commercial Music Library only (business accounts can't use general music).
