    Your PPC Campaign Agent

    This is a working demo of an AI agent that plans, launches and manages pay-per-click campaigns for Friedman's four stores. It studies what sells each season, the weather, stock, and the neighborhoods around each store. Then it proposes a few focused campaigns, writes the ads, watches results every hour and reports every 8 hours. A person approves every dollar it spends.

    What this demo does

    Dashboard

    One screen for all campaigns: totals, what needs a decision, plain-language insights, and a health card for each store.

    Plan

    Best sellers from 3 seasons with a forecast you can review and override. Outside forces such as El Niño, rain, frost, smoke and stock. Audience and buying triggers, where to spend, and optional buyer segments like contractors and new homeowners.

    Campaigns

    3 to 5 proposed campaigns with spend, return and profit ranges you can trace to a source. The ads for every channel, ready for approval. Purchasing and finance confirmations, and the launch checklist.

    Monitor

    Hourly results, 8-hour reports, and suggested adjustments, each with a funnel diagnosis and a fix. Opportunity alerts turn frost, storms, floods or smoke into ready-to-approve flash campaigns.

    Learnings

    What worked, what didn't, every adjustment and decision, and the starting numbers to reuse for the same season next year.

    Sources

    Every data source and what's real, assumed or simulated in this demo.

    Guardrails

    Never without approval

    Raising a budget, bid or cap. New audiences, areas, keywords, channels or ads. Extending a test. Resuming a safety pause.

    Protective actions only

    The agent may pause for a stock-out, a spend cap, an evacuation or unhealthy air, a broken page or lost tracking. Each is reported within the hour.

    Honest numbers

    Every projection shows its formula, inputs and sources as a low, base and high range. Simulated and assumed numbers are labeled wherever they appear.

    Consent and care

    Opted-in lists only, platform-hashed. Texts end with "Reply STOP to opt out." No fear marketing around fires, floods or outages, and never into an evacuation zone.

    Confirmations

    The agent doesn't stop to ask. It answers from the data, sends each answer to its owner, and keeps working. Gate 2 waits for Purchasing to confirm the stock flags on that campaign. Change a working answer and every projection recalculates.

    Purchasing: projected deliveries

    Delivery dates come from Simulated purchase orders and vendor lead times. Stock on hand is Real.

    Finance and marketing

    Ad Samples

    The actual ads for every channel in the proposals, written in Friedman's voice and checked automatically: Google's character limits, TikTok's text limit, a single-text SMS, no prices, no dashes, no unsupported claims, and SHOP plus a category. Approve each one, or send it back with notes. Gate 2 waits until a campaign's ads are approved.

    Learnings

    At the end of every test, and after every decision, the agent saves what happened. Each record holds the conditions, what was projected, what actually happened, the adjustments, and the lessons. Next season, the research step loads the records for the same season and similar conditions. It starts from those real numbers instead of industry benchmarks.

      What the agent took from it

      El Niño is not a sales forecast

      The 2023-24 El Niño season was drier at Santa Rosa (37.6 in.) than the 2022-23 La Niña season (42.6 in.), and Bay Area searches for tarps didn't rise in its Oct to Nov window. Sandbag searches spiked when storms actually hit, in January 2023. The agent plans a normal baseline and lets each forecast storm trigger a burst.

      Rain explains storm sales

      In the backtest, adjusting for actual window rain cut the storm-item miss from about 27% to under 10%. That is why Gutter Gear and Storm Ready run 48-hour bursts off NWS forecasts instead of steady spend.

      Gutter guard is already moving

      Santa Rosa went from 84 Amerimax gutter guard sections on Oct 18 to 71 on Oct 19, ahead of this week's first rain (Real on-hand reads). Soil and frost items barely moved.

      Psychographics Inference

      Preparedness is personal here

      Sonoma County lived through the Tubbs (2017), Kincade (2019) and Glass (2020) fires; Kincade brought the largest evacuation in county history. Messages about getting ready land, but only in a calm, helpful tone.

      Owners with projects

      Petaluma and Sonoma have about 64% owner-occupied homes and median values of $834,900 and $954,300. They spend on the house and the garden, and value advice.

      Smaller households in Sonoma

      About 2 people per household, with the highest per-capita income of the four cities. Likely older; convenience, pickup and a 15-minute consult matter.

      Practical and budget-minded in Ukiah

      Median income $67,122 and a majority of renters. Small, quick fixes and frost protection; strong response to timely, local reminders.

      Bilingual households

      About 29% of Sonoma County and 27% of Mendocino County residents are Hispanic. The agent proposes Spanish ad variants as their own test line, never as targeting by ethnicity.

      Gardeners and growers

      Wine country, clay soil and long falls. Fall planting is a known local habit, and the content agent's planting post already supplies the how-to.

      Gate 3 · Adjustments waiting on you

      Opportunity Alerts

      The agent checks the National Weather Service for every store's zone each hour. When a rule fires, such as frost tonight or severe thunderstorms nearby, it drafts a ready-to-approve flash campaign. Each draft comes with goals, projections, targeting, in-stock products, a budget from the reserve, safety notes and store-ops tasks. Nothing launches without approval unless you've pre-approved that alert type with a spending cap.
